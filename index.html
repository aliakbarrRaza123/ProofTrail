How it works

Issue

The institution adds a student record or uploads a CSV file.

Sign

The system hashes the record and signs it with the institution's key.

Share

The student downloads a PDF certificate with a QR code.

Verify

Anyone scans the QR code and sees Valid, Revoked, or Tampered.

Who uses ProofTrail

Admin

Approves institutions and audits the ledger.

Issuer

Issues and revokes credentials.

Holder

Downloads certificates and shares links.

Verifier

Checks credentials without an account.
